These terms govern authorized use of Nexatech School Operations Platform. A school’s signed service agreement controls if it conflicts with these public terms.

Authorized use

Users may access the service only through an account or demonstration code issued to them and only for legitimate school activities. Credentials and codes must not be shared. Users must promptly report suspected unauthorized access.

School responsibilities

Schools are responsible for the accuracy and lawful collection of submitted information, assigning appropriate roles, maintaining guardian and staff access, providing required privacy notices, and reviewing educational, financial, behaviour, and safeguarding decisions made using the system.

Prohibited conduct

Users must not attempt to cross school boundaries, access another person’s account, bypass security controls, introduce malicious code, scrape the service, disrupt availability, reverse engineer protected components, or use demonstration data or access for operational decisions.

Demonstration access

Demo codes are personal, expiring, and single-use. Demo workspaces are read-only, contain synthetic information, and may be reset or withdrawn without notice. Demonstration access does not create a production subscription.

Suspension and termination

Access may be suspended to protect learners, schools, users, or the platform; to investigate misuse; or when required by law or contract. On termination, export and deletion are handled under the school agreement, retention policy, and applicable law.
