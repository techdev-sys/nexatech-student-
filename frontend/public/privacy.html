Privacy Notice

Effective 20 October 2026

Nexatech School Operations Platform provides software to schools. A participating school normally decides why learner and family information is used and acts as the primary data controller. NEXATECH processes that information to provide the contracted service.

Information we process

Depending on the modules selected by a school, the service may process learner and guardian identity and contact information, enrolment details, attendance, academic results, behaviour records, timetables, communications, invoices, payments, user accounts, and security audit records. Public consultation requests contain the information submitted in the request form.

How information is used

Information is used to operate school workflows, provide authorized portals, communicate with families, maintain records, process requested payments, secure the service, investigate incidents, and meet contractual or legal obligations. We do not use production learner information in public demonstrations.

Access and sharing

Access is restricted by school, role, and relationship to the learner. Information may be handled by contracted hosting, email, SMS, payment, support, and security providers only as needed to deliver the service. Schools determine authorized staff and guardian access.

Retention and security

Operational logs and delivery diagnostics are retained for limited periods. Core school records are retained under the school’s instructions and applicable education, safeguarding, accounting, and legal requirements. We use encrypted transport, access controls, audit logging, backups, and monitoring appropriate to the service.

Your choices and rights

Learners and guardians should normally contact their school to request access, correction, export, restriction, or deletion. Consultation-request users may contact NEXATECH through the contact details on the main site. Some records may need to be retained where law or school obligations require it.

Children’s information

The service is intended for school-authorized educational administration. Schools are responsible for providing required notices, obtaining any necessary consent, and ensuring that accounts and access are appropriate for children and guardians.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. Privacy questions or concerns may be submitted using the contact information published on the Nexatech School Operations Platform home page.
